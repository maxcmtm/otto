Real business owners who use Otto, filming founder-led UGC on their own phone. Never actors, never look-alikes, never
generated people. Three owners per market, each films **two scripts in one session** (about 45 minutes):

## Rules for every creator video (read these to the owner before filming)

1. **Only true statements.** The script is a guide; the owner says it in their own words. Anything that is not true for their business is cut. No numbers that are not in their own Otto report; blur the report if they prefer.
2. **Film after at least a week of real use** (the free trial or a paid plan), so the 07:35 message, the reel and the approvals on screen are theirs.
3. **Disclosure from second one.** NL: on-screen "Betaalde samenwerking met Otto" and the post text starts with it (Reclamecode Social Media & Influencer Marketing). IE: "#Ad" on screen and first in the text (ASAI / CCPC guidance). Run as a Meta partnership ad (Paid partnership label).
4. **Consent in writing** before anything runs: a short release naming the footage, where it runs (Otto's Facebook, Instagram and ads in NL/IE), for how long (12 months), payment, and that they can withdraw for future use. Otto keeps the signed release. The privacy notice's creator row (docs/legal/privacy.md) is written for creators in client videos; have the lawyer extend it to creators in Otto's own ads before the first one runs. The matrix cell gets `"creator": {"name": …, "consent": true}` and `"file"` only after that.
5. **No customers or patients identifiable** without their own written consent; clinics: no treatment claims, no results, no before/after, no patient stories.
6. **Phone, 9:16, daylight, sound on, captions.** Hook in the first two seconds, face to camera. Keep Otto's screen readable in the phone shots (hold 2–3 seconds).
7. **Payment (suggested):** €150 per owner for both takes plus one month of Otto free, paid whether or not the ad runs. Budget: 6 × €150 = €900 across both markets (docs/GO-TO-MARKET-NL-IE.md §2).
