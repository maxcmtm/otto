# Showcase demo brands

The four brands below are **fictional demo brands for Otto's showcase; not clients.** They exist only to show prospects
"the ads Otto makes for your products" on the landing page and in Otto's own Meta ads. No real brand names, logos,
packaging or trade dress were used. A quick web search (2 Oct 2026) found no existing well-known DTC brand under
these exact names in these categories.

All images are AI-generated (Higgsfield, GPT Image 2, 2048 × 2048) and carry an IPTC "trainedAlgorithmicMedia" XMP
mark (platform/otto_provenance.py). Copy rules used: no health or medical claims, no named testimonials, no star
ratings, no invented statistics. The supplement lines are about taste, ritual and convenience only.

Each brand's clean packshot is in `packshots/` and was passed as the reference image for that brand's ads, so the
packaging stays identical. Higgsfield job ids are listed so the packshots can be reused as references.

---

## 1. hushroom: mushroom coffee

- **Category:** mushroom coffee blend (instant powder). Calm morning ritual, pastel and earthy.
- **Fictional demo brand for Otto's showcase; not a client.**
- **Palette:** oat cream `#F2EADB` · sage `#A9BC95` · terracotta `#C8734B` · cocoa brown `#3B2A22`
- **Type:** lowercase soft serif wordmark; small-caps sans for descriptors. Ads use a bold condensed sans or an elegant serif for headlines.
- **Packaging:** matte oat-cream paper tube canister with a matte sage-green lid and a sage band around the base.
  Front: a terracotta line icon of a sun rising over a mushroom cap, the lowercase wordmark "hushroom" in cocoa brown,
  a thin terracotta rule, "MUSHROOM COFFEE", and "Cocoa & Cinnamon · Morning Blend".
- **CTA style:** terracotta pill or rounded rectangle with cream text.
- **Packshot:** `packshots/hushroom-packshot.png` (Higgsfield job `374c2dfc-4e81-4c1f-8e81-d4fefd31df8a`)

## 2. snapleaf: greens gummies

- **Category:** daily greens gummies. Bold, playful, bright.
- **Fictional demo brand for Otto's showcase; not a client.**
- **Palette:** tangerine `#FF7A1A` · lime `#C8F25A` · deep forest green `#0F3D2E` · white `#FFFFFF`
- **Type:** fat, bubbly lowercase wordmark; heavy geometric sans or hand-brushed display for headlines.
- **Packaging:** chunky, squat, glossy tangerine-orange tub with a ribbed deep-forest-green screw lid. Front: the

  (The sprout has two round leaves; nothing pointed or serrated.)
- **CTA style:** tangerine button with forest-green text, or a lime outline pill on dark green.
- **Packshot:** `packshots/snapleaf-packshot.png` (Higgsfield job `e56310a7-ff92-4d9b-a187-e6cc675bd650`)

## 3. AURIVE: premium skincare serum

- **Category:** daily face serum. Clean and clinical-luxe.

## 4. Korrel Bakehouse: neighbourhood bakery-café (NL/IE local business)

- **Category:** local bakery-café (sourdough, croissants, coffee, weekend brunch). Warm and local. Stands in for
  the small owners Otto serves in the Netherlands and Ireland ("korrel" is Dutch for "grain").
- **Fictional demo brand for Otto's showcase; not a client.** No address or opening hours are real.
- **Palette:** flour white `#FAF6EE` · kraft brown `#C49A6C` · tomato red `#D9442B` · rye black `#231F1C`
- **Type:** classic serif in the stamp; very bold condensed sans or slab for headlines.
- **Packaging:** kraft-brown paper bakery bag with a folded top and a tomato-red circular stamp: a single wheat-ear
  icon in a circle, "KORREL" arched above, "BAKEHOUSE" below, and "Fresh every morning" underneath.
- **CTA style:** tomato-red rounded button with white text ("Reserve now", "Book a table", "Order today", "Visit today").
