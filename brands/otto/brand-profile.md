  business; the first week of posts, stories and reels, approvals by e-mail, in Telegram or in the app, the 07:35 report,
  paid ads planned for preview (they launch once a plan is chosen); e-mail two days before the end, nothing charged
  automatically. **Pay-once alternative:** the founding pilot, €197 once (excl. VAT), capped at 50 seats.
- **Next (not live yet, do not advertise until Whop has the plans):** Starter €99 · Growth €249 · Scale €499 a month.

## What Otto sells (outcome, not features)
The month is handled and you stay in control. Otto reads your website, plans the month, writes and designs posts, reels and
ads in your language, asks you only for the yes/no, and at 07:35 every morning tells you what yesterday cost and what it
brought in. Nothing goes out without your tap.

## 😰 Pain points, ranked (owners' own words where possible)
1. "I post when I remember" — no time, no plan, the page goes quiet for weeks.
2. Reels work, but nobody has an evening to film, cut and caption them.
3. Ads spend money and nobody tells you plainly what came back.
4. AI tools still leave the work with you: the idea, the prompt, fixing it, the visual, the posting.
5. Copy that reads like a translation, or like it was written for another country.
6. Fear that an AI posts something wrong or embarrassing in your name.

## ⚡ Purchase triggers
- A season coming (Sinterklaas and Christmas, boiler season, the off-season), a slow week, a new menu or service.
- Seeing a competitor's reels and ads while your own page sits still.
- An agency quote or a freelancer who stopped replying.

## 🚫 Objections + counters
- "Will it look fake or say something wrong?" → Nothing goes out without your tap. Otto builds on your own website and says where a claim comes from; regulated claims get a compliance hold.
- "My business is too specific." → Otto reads your site, prices and competitors and asks at most four questions; every skip and edit teaches it.
- "Is it Dutch, really?" → Written in Dutch for your town, not translated from English.
- "I don't want to pay for something I haven't tried." → 7 days free, no card, nothing charged automatically; or pay once (€197).
- "What about my accounts?" → Official Meta login, revocable any time; Otto never sees your password.
- "Results?" → Ads show numbers in week one; organic takes 60–90 days; the 07:35 report shows both, every morning.
