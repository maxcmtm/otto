## Facts the copy may use (and only these)

- Price: **from €79 a month** (Starter, excl. VAT; yearly = 10 months). Other plans are not public yet; never show their prices.
- Free trial: 7 days, sign in with Google, no card; nothing is charged unless the client chooses a plan.
- Cancel anytime: the client cancels on Otto's Billing page; the plan runs to the end of the paid period.
- Agency prices (research/EU-LAUNCH-AND-PRICING-2026-09.md §3.4, public 2026 price lists): Netherlands €500–1,500 a month for
  an organic entry package, paid social management €500–2,500 or 10–20% of spend; Ireland from €900 (range €450–4,500).
  Say "agencies charge €500–1,500 a month just for posts" or "€1,000+ a month for posts and ads". Never name an agency, never
  claim a saving bigger than these ranges give, put the source line on the landing ("Agency prices: public 2026 price lists
  of social media agencies in the Netherlands and Ireland").
- Speed: the first week is written and designed within minutes of sign-up; reels within 24 hours.
- The 07:35 report: yesterday's posts, ad spend and results, today's schedule, what waits for approval — by e-mail
  (default), Telegram or in the app.
- Post types: feed posts, carousels, stories, reels; pillars like product, how-to, social proof, behind the brand, offers.

## Rules

- No invented customers, testimonials, reviews, logos, results or statistics. No AI-made people. Samples say "Sample".
- No promise of results (sales, followers, ROAS). We promise the work: planned, written, designed, posted, reported.
- Avoid Native's phrases: "autopilot", "paste your website", "walk away".
- Tone: plain, confident, short sentences; English for small-business owners in the Netherlands and Ireland.
