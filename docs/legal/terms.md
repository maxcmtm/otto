## 2. What Otto does

2.1 Otto is an AI marketing service. Once you give it your website address and connect your accounts, Otto:

- reads your public website to build a brand profile: what you sell, how you sound, your colours and your customers' words;
- plans a content calendar and writes and designs posts, stories, reels and ads;
- sends you each post, and each month's paid-ads plan, for approval in the Otto app, by e-mail or in Telegram;
- publishes the posts you approve to your Facebook and Instagram accounts (LinkedIn on request);
- if your plan includes paid ads, creates and runs campaigns in your own Meta and Google Ads accounts within the budgets you approve, reports on them every morning, and pauses campaigns that break the limits you set;
- watches your competitors' public ads and tells you when your results drop.

2.2 What each plan includes (number of brands, posts and reels, ad networks, the monthly band of ad spend we manage, and human support) is shown on our pricing page when you buy.
