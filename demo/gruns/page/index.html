2 · Guardrails

What Otto will not say for Grüns

A US supplement lives under FDA, FTC and Meta rules. Otto wrote 42 rules for Grüns before writing any copy. Every post, card and ad below was checked against them; the Google Search ads were rebuilt because of them.

5 · Reel

The launch reel, rendered

29 seconds, 9:16, voice-over with word-timed captions and a score written for it. Built from the real product: the gummy bear is the thread through every scene. The 11 reels in the calendar are scripted and render the same way once approved.

6 · Ad matrix

Six angles, many styles, half video

Every angle runs as its own ad set with 6–7 different executions, so Meta tests styles inside an angle and angles against each other. Headlines and primary texts belong to the angle and rotate across its visuals. Creator videos are briefs until a real creator films them; Otto never invents a person.
