## 3. The 5 biggest first-month opportunities

1. **Claim "pack vs powder" before AG1 does.** AG1's newest ads push single-serve packs ("One Pack. The Whole Stack.", since Sep 20). Grüns' "All Gummies, No Mess" has run 118 days and the site has real switcher reviews (Bradley O.: "$94 a month… gritty/chalky"). Brief: a creator does rip-tip-eat vs mix-shake-rinse, 15-second reel, switcher quote. No competitor named.
2. **Kids is under-invested.** Only about 31 of 822 Grüns ads mention kids, while Hiya runs 309 ads and its showdown format has lasted 46+ days. Grüns Kids has the stronger story: greens, fiber and 60+ ingredients in one pack for ages 2-13. Run parent-only ads (18+) with picky-eater reviews (Tara, Macall), Sugar-Free first, never "candy". Timed for Halloween (Oct 31).
3. **Make the biggest clusters compliance-proof.** GLP-1 (154 ads) and gut/fiber are the longest earners, but their newest variants invite disapprovals and account risk. Otto runs compliant challengers: third person ("nutrition for people eating less on GLP-1 medication"), a number-led fiber hook ("6 g fiber, one snack pack"), structure/function copy plus the disclaimer. compliance.json already blocks the risky wording.
4. **One clear offer plus a trust message on subscriptions.** Test the transparent "$29.99 first pack, then $49.99 every 4 weeks, pause or cancel anytime, 30-day money back" against the 61% + gifts variant, using Kate S.'s "subscriptions are a trap… resubscribed" as a hook. The goal is lower refund and chargeback risk, auto-renewal-law safety, and a cleaner read on CAC.
5. **Use the Q4 calendar and the Shrek drop.** The Shrek flavor has no ads yet and a real scarcity line. Build a creative bank in October (lower CPMs), run Shrek plus Halloween if the licence allows, scale into BFCM (Nov 26-30), then **January resolutions** (peak supplement demand) and Quitter's Day (Jan 8) with the "the one thing I've been able to stick to" angle.

## 4. Recommended monthly budget split

Grüns (over $300M ARR reported, Unilever-owned) almost certainly spends in the millions a month (not disclosed, unverified). Otto shouldn't take over; it should run a **measured test cell** against business as usual, with a holdout or geo split. Suggested first month: **$60k** (about $2k/day). Scale in January if the CAC target is met (the target is an open ask).

| Channel | Share | $ / month | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meta, prospecting** | 60% | $36k | 6 concept ad sets (angles a1-a6), about 40% new-creative testing and 60% scaling winners. About 20% of prospecting goes to Kids parents. Broad or Advantage+ audiences, existing subscribers excluded. |
| **Meta, retargeting** | 15% | $9k | Catalog ads (evergreen, like Ritual's 158-day and Grüns' 108-day catalog ads) plus objection ads (price per day, pause anytime, RCT) to 1-30 day visitors and add-to-carts. |
| **Google, brand search** | 5% | $3k | Defend "gruns / grüns" terms, promo-code and review queries (cheapest conversions; check auction insights for conquesting). |
| **Google, non-brand search** | 10% | $6k | "greens gummies", "fiber gummies", "vitamins for picky eaters", "greens powder alternative" / "ag1 alternative" (keyword only; the competitor name stays out of ad text). |
| **Google, Performance Max / Shopping** | 10% | $6k | Product feed with brand terms excluded, so it doesn't claim credit for brand demand. |

**Why 75/25 Meta vs Google:** the category is impulse and visual. Taste and format need to be shown, and Meta is where Grüns creates demand (822 active ads). Google mostly captures demand created by Meta, retail (7,000+ doors) and PR, so it gets a smaller, intent-only share.

**Why 80/20 prospecting vs retargeting:** with 1M+ customers and broad retail, warm pools overlap heavily with existing buyers, and Meta's automated campaigns already reach warm users. Retargeting is capped and subscribers are excluded so we don't pay to re-acquire customers.

**Q4:** keep 15-20% of November for Nov 26-30. Front-load testing into October, before BFCM CPM inflation.

**Before launch (asks in strategy.json):** budget and CAC/ROAS target; control offer and BFCM plan; Shrek licence scope and sell-out date; consent for reviews and creators, plus a legal view on GLP-1.
