## 👥 Core audience (creative engine)
- Demographics: US adults 25-55 and parents of kids 2-13. Buyers skew female and mom-led; there is a secondary male segment (Grüns runs "For the Guy Who Won't Take Vitamins"). There is also a large GLP-1-medication segment (about 154 active Grüns ads mention GLP-1).
- Psychographics: they know they "should" take greens or vitamins but have failed at the habit. Powders taste bad and mean mess, pills make them gag, and the bottles pile up. They want something easy and tasty that they won't feel guilty about. They distrust subscriptions.
- Awareness: mostly **solution-aware** (they have tried powders or gummies). Parents and GLP-1 users are **problem-aware**.

Playful, self-aware, a little cheeky. Examples: "We made daily nutrition, like, ridiculously easy.", "a minor sense of superiority", Dr. Barry the Chief Science Officer making puns. ## ⚖️ Compliance (AUTO by industry → brands/gruns/compliance.json)
- US dietary supplement under FDA/DSHEA and FTC. Structure/function claims only, with the FDA disclaimer. No disease claims: cure, treat, prevent, diagnose, heal, "boost immunity against colds/flu", IBS or constipation relief.
- Never "FDA approved". "Made in FDA-registered, cGMP-compliant facilities" is fine.
- No weight-loss promises and no before/after bodies. Grüns' own "Weight Loss Support" and "Support Muscle While You Lose Weight" ads are a risk; Otto won't reproduce them.
- Meta personal-attributes policy: no "Do you suffer…", "Are you tired of…", "Coming off your GLP-1?", "Trying to conceive?". Grüns runs the last two (2026-09-26 and 09-29), and Otto flags them to the client.
- Kids: parents 18+ only. Never target minors. Never call it candy (iron-overdose warning on the label). Licensed characters only within licence and CARU rules.
- Testimonials: verbatim with consent, typical results only, material connections disclosed. gruns.co already notes compensated testimonials, and Shaun White is a paid spokesperson and investor.
- Offers: show the recurring price with any intro price. "Up to X%" must be real. Deadlines and "no restocks" must be true.
- Prop 65 lead warning on gruns.co, so never "lead-free" or "no heavy metals". Never "Made in USA" alone (USA & Canada).
- The how-it-works page claims ("hormone health", "better metabolism", "achieving peak health") are too strong for ads. Don't reuse them.
