How the simulation works

One model, run per day for 90 days from 1 October 2026. Organic reach = followers × 18% × a format multiplier (reel 3.2, carousel 1.6, post 1.0) × a learning lift that compounds 0.6% a day and stops at +45%.

    Doubts, and Otto's answer to each

    “Does CBD even work?”
    Education and the entourage-effect explainer. Never a medical claim, only “may support”.
    “Is it legal? Will it make me intoxicated?”
    The THC-free broad-spectrum and isolate lines, and the lab certificates.
    “Too expensive.”
    Dosing maths, cost per day, and bundle value.
    “Which product is for me?”
    Spectrum-picker content, quiz-style posts.

    The check at work, on a line from Otto's own week-1 draft

    DraftMyth: “CBD gets you high.” Fact: CBD is non-intoxicating.
    Ships asMyth: “CBD is intoxicating.” Fact: CBD is non-intoxicating, and the broad-spectrum and isolate lines contain no THC.

    On day three the work starts arriving. Each post comes to the owner as a card in Telegram with the image, the caption, the slot and the reason for it. Approve, skip, ask for an edit, or leave it for tomorrow.

    Days4–29

    Otto publishes. The owner reads one message a morning.

    A report at 07:35, an alert when something drops, a fix already drafted, and a weekly look at the competition.

    Simulated

    From day four the plan runs itself. Approved posts go out on their slot; the owner's side of the job is one message each morning.

    When a number falls 30% or more below its 7-day average, Otto says so the same day and drafts a fix the owner can approve with one tap. Every Friday it names what is winning and moves next week's plan toward it. Every Monday it checks the four competitors.

    The morning report on the right follows the day bar. Scroll, or drag the bar, and watch it change.

    Assumptions behind these numbers

    Start at 1,200 followers. Reach per post = followers × 18% × format (reel 3.2, carousel 1.6, post 1.0), plus a learning lift of 0.6% a day. 0.35% of people reached follow; 0.6% click through. Weekends carry one post, weekdays two.
