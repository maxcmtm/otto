The confusion: CBD labels say "full spectrum", "broad spectrum", "isolate". Most buyers do not know the difference,
and many worry about THC ("will it get me high?", "will it show up?").

Full spectrum: the whole hemp plant's profile working together — CBD plus the minor cannabinoids (CBG, CBN),
terpenes, and naturally occurring trace THC within the legal limit. People call this the "entourage effect":
the compounds working as a team.

Broad spectrum: the same team, minus THC. You keep CBD, CBG, CBN and terpenes; THC is removed.
For people who want the whole-plant profile with zero THC.

Isolate: pure CBD, nothing else. No other cannabinoids, no terpenes, no THC. For people who want only CBD.

How to choose: want the whole plant → full. Whole plant but zero THC → broad. Only CBD → isolate.
