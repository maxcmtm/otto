## Intent

Otto's reference explainer reel for Happy Garden (happygardeneu.com, EU CBD store). A 40-second,
motion-design-led vertical explainer that teaches the three CBD spectrums in plain words and makes
the brand the trustworthy guide. Calm, clear, premium — closer to an Apple product explainer than a
supplement ad. It must look like a studio made it: kinetic type, a real diagram that builds, brand
colors, no stock-video feel, no slideshow of photos.

## Customizations

- Brand palette from the live site scan: yellow #FFBC00 (recognition color), deep teal #0E252C,
  hemp green #31856C, pink accent #F95C8F, white.
- One visual system for the three spectrums: a "molecule set" (CBD, CBG, CBN, terpenes, THC trace)
  that visibly loses members as we go Full → Broad → Isolate. The subtraction IS the explanation.
- End card: Happy Garden wordmark, "Every batch third-party lab tested", link in bio.
- Voice-over: calm female, ElevenLabs (via Higgsfield), ~145 wpm. Music: soft organic underscore.

## Notes

- Compliance (Meta + EU): no medical or cure claims, no "treats/heals", no THC promotion. Allowed:
  what each spectrum contains, lab testing, choosing by preference. "May support" only if needed.
- Every claim must exist on the brand's site/profile: third-party lab testing, GMP facilities,
  Full/Broad/Isolate definitions, THC-free Broad and Isolate lines.
- This project is also the golden reference for Otto's automated reel pipeline (skills/otto-motion-director).
