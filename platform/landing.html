Questions

Still unsure? Scan your site first. It’s free and takes a minute.

How much does Otto cost?
Otto is free for 7 days, with no card. After that, Otto Starter is €79 a month, excluding VAT: a monthly subscription, no contract, cancel anytime. Your ad budget is separate: you pay it to Meta directly, and nothing is spent on ads until you approve it.
What’s included in the €79?
Everything Otto does for one business: up to 50 posts and carousels, 12 stories and 4 reels a month, made from your website and in your brand; Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram, planned, made and run, with 20 new ads a month and up to €1,000 a month of ad budget managed; approvals by e-mail, in the app or in Telegram; the 07:35 report every morning; and a monthly look at your competitors.
Does anything publish without my approval?
No. Every post and every ad waits for your approval, by e-mail, in Telegram or in the app, whichever is closer. You can let trusted pillars publish automatically later; it is off by default.
How is this different from a scheduler plus ChatGPT?
With those tools you do the work: you think of the idea, write the prompt, fix the output, make the visual, pick the slot, press publish and check the stats. Otto does all of that and comes to you only with finished posts and decisions.
Will the content actually sound like my brand?
Otto builds your brand profile from your own site: your colours, your wording, your real customer reviews. It asks you at most four questions to fill the gaps. Every post and visual follows that profile, and every post you approve or skip teaches it more.
What if I’m not around to approve?
Nothing breaks. Otto reminds you before a post is due and moves it to a new slot if you don’t answer. Your morning briefing lists what is waiting.
Which channels and languages?
Facebook and Instagram, for posts and ads. Content in English and Dutch, written for your market rather than translated, and in German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. Your approvals and the 07:35 report come in English, Dutch or German.
What do I need to bring?
Your website address, your social accounts and your Meta ad account, connected once through their official login (you can revoke access at any time), and an e-mail address for approvals (or Telegram, if you prefer).
What happens after the free trial?
Otto e-mails you two days before your 7-day trial ends. Choose Starter to keep going: it starts, and is charged, when the trial ends, and your setup and everything Otto made stay. If you don’t, nothing is charged, publishing pauses and your work is kept for 90 days.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. It is a monthly subscription with no contract: cancel on Otto’s Billing page whenever you like. Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for, and nothing is charged after that.
