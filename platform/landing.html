Before you decide

Fair questions.

Does anything publish without my approval?
No. Every post waits for your yes — in Telegram or in the dashboard, whichever is closer. Later you can let trusted pillars auto-publish; it's off by default.
How is this different from a scheduler plus ChatGPT?
Those wait for you: you think of the idea, write the prompt, fix the output, design the visual, pick the slot, press publish, check the stats. Otto owns that entire loop and comes to you only with finished work and decisions. The direction of work is reversed — that's the product.
Will the content actually sound like my brand?
Otto builds your brand profile from your own site: your palette, your phrasing, your real customer reviews, your proof. Then it asks you at most four questions to close the gaps. That profile binds every post and visual — and every approve or skip teaches it your taste.
What if I'm not around to approve?
Nothing breaks. Otto reminds you before a slot is missed, and reschedules if you don't answer. Your morning briefing lists exactly what's waiting.
Which channels and languages?
Facebook and Instagram today, LinkedIn on request. Content in English, German and Hebrew, including right-to-left layouts — we run a Hebrew-language brand on Otto right now.
What do I need to bring?
Your website URL, your social accounts connected once via official login (revoke anytime), and Telegram on your phone. That's the whole setup.
What happens after the pilot?
Founding customers keep their environment and get grandfathered terms when Otto moves to monthly plans. Early is the good side of this trade.
