Step 3 · Your approval

Nothing is posted until you approve it.

Every post arrives in Telegram exactly as it will appear in the feed. Approve it, skip it, or reply “make it shorter” and Otto rewrites it. The same posts wait in your dashboard, so you can decide wherever you are.

Step 4 · Publishing and alerts

Otto tells you when something changes.

Approved posts go out on schedule. Otto compares reach, clicks and leads with your own averages, sends a short briefing every morning, and messages you when something drops, with a fix already drafted. You decide; Otto does the work.

Step 5 · Ads and reports

A short report every morning, and the month at a glance.

Connect your Meta and Google ad accounts and Otto plans campaigns on the same calendar as your posts, sends a daily report on spend and results, and shows month-over-month growth: reach, engagement, followers, spend and leads.

Questions

Does anything publish without my approval?
No. Every post waits for your approval, in Telegram or in the dashboard, whichever is closer. You can let trusted pillars publish automatically later; it is off by default.
How is this different from a scheduler plus ChatGPT?
With those tools you do the work: you think of the idea, write the prompt, fix the output, make the visual, pick the slot, press publish and check the stats. Otto does all of that and comes to you only with finished posts and decisions.
Will the content actually sound like my brand?
Otto builds your brand profile from your own site: your colours, your wording, your real customer reviews. It asks you at most four questions to fill the gaps. Every post and visual follows that profile, and every post you approve or skip teaches it more.
What if I’m not around to approve?
Nothing breaks. Otto reminds you before a post is due and moves it to a new slot if you don’t answer. Your morning briefing lists what is waiting.
Which channels and languages?
Facebook and Instagram today, LinkedIn on request. Content in English, German and Hebrew, including right-to-left layouts. One of the brands running on Otto today publishes in Hebrew.
What do I need to bring?
Your website address, your social accounts connected once through their official login (you can revoke access at any time), and Telegram on your phone.
What happens after the pilot?
You keep your setup, and your founding terms carry over when Otto moves to monthly plans.
